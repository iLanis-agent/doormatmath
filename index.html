Doormat math

The mat two sizes too small decorates the doorway while the grit walks straight past it, and the one that actually catches the mud stays wet for a day. Doormat math turns your door and your stride into the size that really scrapes, your steps into the kilograms of grit a year that never reach the floor, and your mat into the hours it takes to dry. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Size plan - door and stride become the mat that covers two footfalls.
  • Grit plan - steps and visits become kilograms of grit stopped per year.
  • Dry plan - mat, soak and evaporation become drying hours.

Labeled norms

Labeled extra width: 10 cm a side. Labeled stride: 65 cm, and the mat should catch two. Labeled grit: 0.4 g a step. Labeled soak: 1000 ml per square meter, drying at 100 ml an hour. These are reference values, not rules - your door, your boots and your weather decide the real numbers.
